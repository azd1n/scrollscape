How to play

1

Snap

Take a photo of anything. It is cut into tiles and shuffled, with no decoys. Every tile belongs somewhere on the board.

2

Place

Tap the cell where the tile belongs. It previews there. Then double-tap the tile to lock it in. A wrong cell is a strike, three strikes end the run, and a swipe up skips a tile.

3

Turn up the heat

Beat the clock: no strikes, a wrong cell just bounces, but the timer runs. Twisted tiles: every tile arrives turned, so rotate it upright before you lock it.

4

Race friends

Make a room and share the code. One person snaps, everyone races, the first to finish wins and snaps the next photo.
